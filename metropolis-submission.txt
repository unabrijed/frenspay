4. PROBLEM, SOLUTION AND USEFULNESS - COPY THIS ANSWER
------------------------------------------------------------

Holding stablecoins and knowing how to use them are different things.
For someone encountering crypto payments for the first time, wallet
addresses, networks, funding and transaction approvals can feel unfamiliar,
even when scanning a payment QR is already second nature.

Frenspay is building a familiar way to learn that journey: an invite-only,
USDC-funded payment simulation built on Monad.

Users sign in with email and receive an embedded wallet. Real native USDC
held in their wallet on Monad mainnet unlocks a test spending allowance.
They can then scan an Indian UPI QR, review its payee and INR amount, and
complete a clearly labelled simulation that earns points and badges.

These simulations do not transfer funds to merchants or settle UPI payments.
The user's USDC remains in their wallet unless they explicitly withdraw it.
Simulation activity and points are recorded offchain in our application
ledger. No payment or receipt transaction is sent to a blockchain.

The allowance follows a peak-balance model: the highest verified USDC balance
multiplied by a fixed 10 determines cumulative simulation credits. Spending
consumes those credits; withdrawing and redepositing the same balance does
not replenish them. There are no free starting credits, daily resets or
promises that points will become valuable tokens.

Our initial goal is to validate the complete consumer journey: email
onboarding, funding, QR scanning, understandable confirmations and wallet
recovery. Live merchant settlement will require an eligible payment provider.

Monad provides the real-USDC wallet infrastructure for this beta. The application
verifies mainnet USDC balances and records simulations offchain, while
keeping simulated activity separate from real funds.

The implementation includes strict UPI QR parsing, server-side credit
accounting, retry protection against duplicate debits, withdrawal recovery,
invite controls and an operator dashboard. The current build has passed
isolated database and browser tests; production sponsorship and real
deposit/withdrawal acceptance testing remain release gates.

5. FIRST USERS AND DISTRIBUTION - COPY THIS ANSWER
------------------------------------------------------------

Our first users are a small, invited group of crypto-curious friends and
existing stablecoin users familiar with Indian UPI QR payments. We want
people who can tell us where a USDC wallet feels confusing and whether a
familiar scan-and-confirm interaction makes the experience easier to
understand.

We will recruit the first cohort through the founder's personal network
and direct outreach to Monad community members. Onboarding will be hands-on:
help users enter through an invitation, explain the distinction between
real USDC and simulated spending, and observe where they need assistance.

Once the initial journey works reliably, we will expand through member
invitations, short product demonstrations on X and community feedback
sessions. Points and badges provide a reason to explore the experience,
without promising financial rewards.

We will measure invited-to-activated conversion, verified funding,
completion of a first simulation, return usage on separate days, and
successful wallet recovery. Internal and demonstration accounts will be
excluded from usage reporting.

QR metadata and simulations are not evidence of actual merchant purchases
or physical visits. We will report product engagement separately from
real payment volume.

We are at the implementation and pilot-preparation stage. We are not
claiming an established user base, merchant adoption or live UPI settlement.
